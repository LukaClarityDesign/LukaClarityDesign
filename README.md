# Bartosz Łuka — Luka Clarity Design (L.C.D.)

**Digital Designer & Android/Kotlin Developer | UX/UI | Founder, Luka Clarity Design | Genk, Belgium**

Clarity for complex ideas.

I design and build digital products myself: from the interface to working code. I am based in Genk, in Belgian Limburg. L.C.D. is my personal brand and own project for digital design, UX/UI, visual communication and product development — not a registered company.

Document screening and structured checks against Belgian remuneration rules; interface in Dutch, English, Polish and other languages.
